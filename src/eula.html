scKillboard
What this program does

Welcome to scKillboard. Here's exactly what this program does, in plain terms — please read it before continuing.

Why it exists

scKillboard is built to promote PvP in Star Citizen — to help organizations recruit active players, help pilots find each other through the LFG board, and bring the community together around the fight. It's a tool for the players, by the players.

What it reads

scKillboard reads only two files on your computer:

• Your Star Citizen Game.log file
• Its own settings file in your AppData folder

That's it — nothing else on your system is read or touched.

What it records from your Game.log

From the log it detects and records your combat activity: kills and deaths, the vehicle you're inside at the time, the weapon you're carrying, bounty missions completed, and your location in the 'verse. This is what builds your killboard, stats, and leaderboard rank.

How it uses RSI data

For each kill and death, scKillboard looks up the players involved on their public RSI profile pages — the same pages anyone can view in a browser — to show avatars, organizations, and profile details next to the fight. It reads only public information and never uses your RSI login or password.

Supporting the game

scKillboard is an unofficial, fan-made tool made to support Star Citizen and its PvP community. It is not affiliated with Cloud Imperium Games. We fully respect CIG's ownership of the game and will comply with any instruction or request from Cloud Imperium Games regarding this tool.
